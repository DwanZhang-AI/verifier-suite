提示: ## Rounding policy All credit card rewards points are rounded down to the nearest whole number. When calculating points earned on a purchase, the system always truncates (floors) fractional points rather than rounding to the nearest integer. For example, a $126.77 purchase on a 1% cash back card earns 126 points (not 127), because 126.77 × 0.01 = 1.2677 points, which rounds down to 1 point per dollar, giving 126 points. Similarly, a $99.99 purchase at 2.5% cash back would calculate to 249.975 points but would award 249 points.
